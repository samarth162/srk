BRIEF

A quick debrief on the astronaut before liftoff.

Hi — I'm Samarth. A final-year Aerospace Engineering student at Amity University Maharashtra, Mumbai, with a passion for the intersection of space structures, advanced materials, and sustainable innovation.

My research interests gravitate toward reconfigurable space structures, advanced composite materials, In-Space Manufacturing, and structural optimization — supported by proficiency in FEA, CAD, and CFD.

I've had the privilege of interning at ISRO's SDSC-SHAR (working on S200 strap-on boosters of LVM3) and DRDO's Composite Research Centre in Pune. I also hold a Business Administration minor — because the best aerospace innovations need visionary builders too.

Inspired by the legacies of Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam and Sir Ratan Tata, I believe technology earns its worth when it creates tangible value for humanity.
